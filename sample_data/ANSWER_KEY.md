# TalentIQ Sample Data Answer Key

## Expected ranking

The expected order is:

1. Priya Nair
2. Ananya Das
3. Meera Joshi
4. Rohan Verma
5. Sneha Iyer
6. Vikram Singh
7. Karthik Reddy

The model may produce different numeric scores, but strong candidates should remain
above candidates with limited or unsupported experience.

## Demo traps

* Ananya Das describes relevant skills as T-SQL, dataframes, and Microsoft's business
  intelligence dashboard suite. The screener should recognize the equivalent evidence.
* Vikram Singh lists many keywords but has only six months of vague internship experience.
  The skills list alone should not produce a high score.
* Market insights without supplied public job-post text must be labelled as estimates.

All names, companies, email addresses, and phone numbers are fictional.
